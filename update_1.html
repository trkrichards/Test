Commercial Readiness — Weekly Status Report

Pull from your Outlook email and calendar, Teams chats, prior status reports, and meeting notes from the past 7 days. For each project you touched this week, write 1–4 sentences in third person, starting with your name (e.g. “Barry finalized…”). State what you did and the current status — no filler, no speculation. Leave any project blank if you have nothing to report. Submitting adds your entry to this week's shared report — it does not overwrite anyone else's.

How this works: your update is written to the weeklyReports/<week-of> record in Firestore, alongside (not overwriting) anyone else's entry for the same week. A permanent, read-only copy of every submission is also kept in that week's history log. The combined report on index.html reads directly from Firestore, so it updates as soon as you submit — no commits or redeploys needed. The local backup download is just a safety copy in case of a connection issue.
